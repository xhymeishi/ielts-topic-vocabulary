# IELTS and practical English topic map

Use this as a menu, not a mandatory syllabus. Select a focused subtopic for each lesson; rotate among families only when the learner requests rotation or has not selected a theme.

| Topic family | Example subtopics | Useful contexts |
|---|---|---|
| Education and learning | online learning, assessment, lifelong learning, vocational skills | IELTS Reading, Writing Task 2, study and training |
| Work and careers | remote work, workplace culture, recruitment, career development | job interviews, meetings, professional communication |
| Business and the economy | entrepreneurship, consumer behavior, inflation, productivity, trade | IELTS social/economic topics, business writing |
| Technology and AI | automation, generative AI, data privacy, digital access, innovation | AI work, product discussions, IELTS technology topics |
| Blockchain and Web3 | digital assets, decentralised systems, governance, security, token incentives | Web3 work and industry conversations; explain technical terms simply |
| Environment and energy | climate policy, renewable energy, conservation, sustainable cities | IELTS essays and science reporting |
| Health and wellbeing | public health, healthcare access, mental health, nutrition | IELTS Reading and social issues |
| Society and culture | inequality, migration, media influence, community, changing lifestyles | IELTS Writing Task 2 and discussion |
| Cities and transport | urbanisation, housing, public transit, infrastructure, tourism | IELTS maps, essays, and practical travel language |
| Science and research | evidence, methodology, discovery, limitations, public funding | IELTS academic reading and explaining research |
| Media and communication | journalism, social platforms, advertising, misinformation, audience growth | content creation and professional communication |
| Government and public policy | regulation, taxation, public services, incentives, implementation | IELTS social topics and formal discussion |
| Custom theme | any learner-selected field, project, or current interest | Tailor vocabulary and examples to the stated goal |

For a scheduled rotation, alternate between requested topic families and vary subtopics. Let current learner goals influence examples, but keep the skill useful to learners with different careers and interests.
