# Example: compact article lesson

This example illustrates the default 8-item lesson shape. It is a format sample, not official IELTS material or a required fixed word list.

## Why reliable public transport matters

For many people, the daily **commute** is one of the most stressful parts of urban life. When buses and trains are **reliable**, workers can plan their day with confidence. A well-connected network also gives residents better **access to** jobs, schools, and health services. Although improving public transport requires substantial **investment**, it can help **reduce** road **congestion** and make travel more **affordable**. Over time, a shift away from private cars may support a more **sustainable** city.

## Vocabulary

1. **commute** *(noun/verb)* — 通勤；通勤往返 — *a daily commute* — Her commute takes about forty minutes.
2. **reliable** *(adjective)* — 可靠的 — *a reliable service* — Passengers need reliable buses during peak hours.
3. **access** *(noun)* — 使用或获得某物的机会 — *access to healthcare* — Public transport improves access to local services.
4. **investment** *(noun)* — 投资；投入 — *investment in infrastructure* — The city announced new investment in rail lines.
5. **reduce** *(verb)* — 减少 — *reduce travel time* — Dedicated bus lanes can reduce delays.
6. **congestion** *(noun)* — 拥堵 — *traffic congestion* — Better transit may ease congestion downtown.
7. **affordable** *(adjective)* — 负担得起的 — *affordable fares* — Discounted fares make travel more affordable for students.
8. **sustainable** *(adjective)* — 可持续的 — *sustainable transport* — Cycling and public transit can support sustainable urban growth.

## Quick recall

1. Which adjective means “can be trusted to work well”?
2. Complete: “The city needs more investment ___ public transport.”
3. Give one phrase that commonly follows **congestion**.
4. Make a sentence using **affordable**.

---

**Answers**

1. reliable
2. in
3. traffic congestion
4. Answers will vary; check that the sentence uses **affordable** to mean “not too expensive.”
